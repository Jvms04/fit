## Authorized protocol

The exploratory fixture contains 1,000 deterministic rows and is explicitly not an approved Alpha volume. Warm preparation exits the activity with `KEYCODE_BACK`; the sample is accepted only when Android reports `LaunchState: WARM` and a finite non-negative `TotalTime`.

The runner refuses 30 samples so this stage cannot silently become `VAL-001`. Raw samples must be retained before summaries. `UNKNOWN (0)` is `NOT_A_LAUNCH_EVENT`; an unexpected state or absent `TotalTime` makes the attempt diagnostic-only. `WaitTime` is preserved raw and never substituted for `TotalTime`.
